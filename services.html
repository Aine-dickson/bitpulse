How an engagement runs

Four stages, each one gating the next.

01 · SCOPE

Acquire

We pin the problem, constraints and success signal before anyone touches a schematic.

02 · DESIGN

Route

Architecture, board and protocol are drawn, reviewed against the spec, then priced.

03 · BUILD

Fabricate

Firmware, hardware and services built in demoable slices, with a test rig at every step.

04 · SUSTAIN

Monitor

We instrument, hand over, and keep the system observable long after switch-on.
